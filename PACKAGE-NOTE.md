# P18 — Package Note (Fiverr-ready)

**Gig title:** I will analyse your customer reviews and tell you what customers really think

**What the client gets:** a review sentiment report for their
online store — how customers actually feel (from the words they
wrote, not just the stars), which product, category and channel
draws the complaints, what the complaints are about (counted from
the text), where the stars disagree with the words, and three
prioritised fixes with the numbers attached. Delivered as a 2-page
PDF report plus a live Excel workbook they can re-run on next
month's reviews themselves.

## Tiers (Hashir's band)

| Tier | Price | Delivery | Includes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Basic | $20 | 2 days | The 2-page PDF report on up to 500 of their reviews: sentiment mix, worst products, top complaint themes |
| Standard | $65 | 3 days | Everything in Basic + up to 2,000 reviews, sentiment by category/channel/month, the sentiment-vs-stars cross-tab, the live Excel workbook with formulas |
| Premium | $140 | 5 days | Everything in Standard + up to 5,000 reviews, the 3 recommendations with the numbers attached, a reusable scoring template (open lexicon they can edit) for their own future exports, 1 revision round |

**What's NOT included:** collecting or scraping their reviews
(they export them from their store, Daraz or Meta — quoted
separately if they need scraping); replying to reviewers or
managing their reputation (that's a different service); a machine-learning
sentiment model (this service uses a transparent, editable word-list
lexicon — the client can see exactly why every review scored the
way it did, which is the point).

**Delivery time note:** counts from the day the client uploads their
review export, not from order placement.

**Proof this service works:** this repo's sample — BazaarHub.pk,
780 reviews over 6 months, 52.31% positive against 27.82% negative,
AirBuds Pro found at 46.27% negative (worst of 15 products),
Damaged product the top complaint in 121 reviews, Daraz the most
negative channel at 32.96% against 20.65% on WhatsApp / Instagram,
stars and text disagreeing in 79 reviews (including 5 five-star
reviews with negative text), negative sentiment down 8.82 points
from January to June, 169 automated checks passing, and a 2-page PDF
the buyer sees before ordering.
